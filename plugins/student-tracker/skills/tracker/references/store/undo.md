# Store: retract, delete, sensitive flip

- **`retractEntry(eid?)`** → `{entry, trainees, retracted: [ids]}`. The named Entry, or the last written (latest `at`, then id; never reaching past a retracted one). Flags the Entry and every record cut from it (in `sensitive/` too) retracted and rebuilds cards. Nothing deleted. Other Entries' records stay: a Follow-up it closed reopens; Assessments citing it lose it from `n`; its Status, stage, Snapshot, retirements and items fall back to the earlier ones. Refuses an unknown id, an already retracted Entry, or no Entry at all.
- **`hardDelete(eid)`** → `{entry, trainees, deleted: [ids]}`. Permanently deletes the Entry (retracted or not) and everything cut from it, `sensitive/` included, and rebuilds cards; Assessments elsewhere keep a dangling id (out of `n`). Only on the Commander's explicit request, after confirming; never on "בטל". Refuses without an id or an unknown id.
- **`flipSensitive(id, category?)`** → `{id, now: "public"|"sensitive", moved: [ids], repeats?}`. Only on the Commander's word ("זה רגיש" / "זה לא רגיש"). Moves one Evidence, with the Follow-ups it opened and the items taken from it, between `sensitive/` and the public records, same ids, with the card. Into `sensitive/` needs `category` (a category, or `other`). A public Evidence it came from keeps its marker. Refuses a Follow-up or item id (flip its Evidence), a non-Evidence id, a retracted Evidence. Into `sensitive/`, `repeats: [{what, text}]` lists what the card still shows that is no longer display-safe (`what`: `snapshot`, `nextTalk` per question, `status`/`left` reason, `item`). Older records aren't re-checked.
